Popular Diagnostic Tests

CBC (Complete Blood Count)

💰 Price: ₹450

Sample: Blood

Fasting: Not Required

TAT: 4 hours

This test evaluates overall health by measuring red cells, white cells, hemoglobin, and platelets. Helps detect anemia, infections, and blood disorders.

MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)

💰 Price: ₹2500

Sample: Imaging (Non-invasive)

Fasting: May be Required

TAT: 24 hours

MRI uses magnetic fields and radio waves to create detailed images of organs and tissues. Used for brain, spine, joints, and soft tissue evaluation.

Blood Sugar (Glucose)

💰 Price: ₹500

Sample: Blood

Fasting: Required

TAT: 2 hours

This test checks glucose levels to diagnose and monitor diabetes and hypoglycemia. Includes FBS, PPBS, and RBS variations. Involves finger prick or blood draw from your vein.

Lipid Profile

💰 Price: ₹1000

Sample: Blood

Fasting: 9–12 hours Required

TAT: 6 hours

This test measures total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, and triglycerides to assess heart health and risk of cardiovascular disease.

Thyroid Profile (T3, T4, TSH)

💰 Price: ₹900

Sample: Blood

Fasting: Not Required

TAT: 6 hours

Measures TSH, T3, and T4 to assess thyroid function. Helps detect hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, and monitor thyroid treatment.

Vitamin D (25-OH)

💰 Price: ₹1250

Sample: Blood

Fasting: Not Required

TAT: 12 hours

This test determines 25-hydroxyvitamin D levels to assess bone health, immune function, and calcium absorption. Helps detect deficiency and monitor supplementation.

Liver Function Test (LFT)

💰 Price: ₹700

Sample: Blood

Fasting: 8–10 hours Required

TAT: 6 hours

This test measures liver enzymes, bilirubin, and proteins to assess liver health, detect liver diseases, and monitor ongoing treatment.

HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)

💰 Price: ₹500

Sample: Blood

Fasting: Not Required

TAT: 4 hours

This test measures the average blood sugar over the past 2–3 months to diagnose and monitor diabetes, helping assess long-term glucose control.

COVID-19 RT-PCR

💰 Price: ₹800

Sample: Nasal/Throat Swab

Fasting: Not Required

TAT: 12–24 hours

This test detects SARS-CoV-2 virus using RT-PCR technology for accurate diagnosis of active COVID-19 infection. Recommended for symptomatic patients, travel, or exposure screening.

Kidney Function Test (KFT)

💰 Price: ₹900

Sample: Blood

Fasting: 8–10 hours Required

TAT: 6 hours

Measures BUN, creatinine, uric acid, and electrolytes to assess kidney health, diagnose kidney disease, and monitor ongoing treatment.

ECG (Electrocardiogram)

💰 Price: ₹400

Sample: Heart Electrical Activity

Fasting: Not Required

TAT: Immediate

Records the heart’s electrical signals to detect irregular rhythms, heart attacks, and other cardiac conditions. Quick, painless, and non-invasive.

Urine Routine & Microscopy

💰 Price: ₹350

Sample: Urine

Fasting: Not Required

TAT: 4 hours

Examines urine's physical, chemical, and microscopic properties to detect UTIs, kidney disorders, and metabolic conditions.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

➕Is fasting needed for lipid test?

Yes, 10–12 hours fasting is advised for accurate results for some tests.

➕Are digital reports provided?

Yes, PDF reports are emailed and visible on your dashboard.

➕Can I reschedule my appointment?

Appointments can be rescheduled at least 4 hours in advance.

➕What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, cards, net banking, and cash on collection.

➕How long does it take to get results?

Most reports are available within 24 hours; some tests may take 2–3 days.

➕Are home sample collections available?

Yes, home sample collection is available in select areas.

➕Do I need to bring documents?

Bring valid ID and doctor prescription (if required).

➕Are test results confidential?

Yes, all reports are securely stored and accessible only to you.

Insurance & Payments
